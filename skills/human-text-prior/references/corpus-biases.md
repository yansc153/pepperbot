# Corpus Biases

This skill was grounded in a WeChat group-chat corpus at `/Users/oxjames/Downloads/texts`.

The corpus is useful, but it is not neutral.

Use it as a source of text mechanics, not as a universal social voice.

## What The Corpus Is Good For

It is strong evidence for:

- contemporary Chinese short-form compression
- stance-first phrasing
- uneven sentence weight
- local self-repair
- high-context writing
- modern informal rhythm

## What The Corpus Is Not

It is not a safe template for:

- corporate copy
- female lifestyle voice
- regulated content
- public statements
- knowledge-base articles
- legal or medical explanations

## Observed Biases

### 1. Domain bias

A large share of the corpus lives in:

- AI
- coding tools
- crypto
- Web3
- markets
- group banter

Do not let those domains leak into unrelated tasks.

### 2. Register bias

Many messages are:

- sharper
- more male-coded
- more teasing
- more blunt

That does not mean all modern human text should sound like that.

### 3. Noise bias

The corpus contains:

- spam
- recruiting posts
- service ads
- finance pitches
- group migration notices
- media placeholders

These are part of the raw environment, not part of the desired writing prior.

### 4. Reply artifact bias

Some lines depend on quoted context and look strange alone.

Do not treat context-dependent fragments as standalone style targets.

### 5. Hype bias

Some source groups are dominated by speculation, hype, and reaction loops.

That can teach urgency and compression, but it can also distort judgment.

## Safe Transfer Rules

### Always transfer

- compression
- faster point arrival
- lower transition density
- mixed sentence length
- more believable thought movement

### Transfer only on scene match

- slang
- strong teasing
- profanity
- insider acronyms
- heavy English mixing
- market jargon

### Never transfer by default

- spam framing
- pitch voice
- compensation talk
- hyper-bullish asset language
- random sexual or crude jokes
- quote-thread residue

## Guardrail

If a rewrite becomes more vivid but less appropriate for the surface, you crossed the line.

Back off one strength level and keep only the structural gains.

## Recommended Neutral Build

If you want this skill to behave more like a general human-text layer and less like a tech-market artifact, rebuild the filtered corpus with:

- `--exclude-domain ai`
- `--exclude-domain crypto`
- `--exclude-domain web3`

That does not make the corpus universal. It just removes three of the loudest bias sources.
