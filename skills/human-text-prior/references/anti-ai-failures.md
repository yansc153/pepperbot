# Anti AI Failures

This file lists the recurring failure modes that make Chinese writing feel model-native instead of human-native.

Fix the structure first. Do not start by swapping adjectives.

## 1. Background Dump Opening

Symptom:

- the draft starts by re-explaining the full topic before taking a position

Why it feels fake:

- real people usually do not open like a workshop facilitator

Fix:

- move the stance or observation to sentence one
- keep only the background that changes the meaning

## 2. Thesis Essay Skeleton

Symptom:

- "首先 / 其次 / 最后"
- "从 A、B、C 三个方面来看"
- every paragraph feels pre-outlined

Why it feels fake:

- it sounds like school formatting, not live thought

Fix:

- collapse the skeleton
- group by momentum, not by textbook order

## 3. Over-Symmetry

Symptom:

- every sentence has matching length
- too many neat pairs like "不是 X，而是 Y"
- too many three-item lists

Why it feels fake:

- humans are not that balanced in ordinary writing

Fix:

- break the symmetry
- keep only the contrast that carries actual meaning

## 4. Explanation Before Position

Symptom:

- the model spends several lines warming up before saying what it thinks

Why it feels fake:

- modern real text is more positional

Fix:

- say what the writer thinks earlier
- let explanation serve the stance, not delay it

## 5. Total Context Syndrome

Symptom:

- every noun gets reintroduced
- every reference gets unpacked
- nothing is allowed to remain shared context

Why it feels fake:

- humans assume more common ground

Fix:

- remove context that the audience already has
- leave some things implied if the meaning stays stable

## 6. Abstract Noun Overload

Symptom:

- too many words like "趋势、格局、路径、逻辑、价值、意义"
- verbs get replaced by abstract framing

Why it feels fake:

- the text sounds like it wants to sound thoughtful

Fix:

- replace abstract framing with concrete actions, reactions, or stakes

## 7. Smoothness Addiction

Symptom:

- every transition is polished
- sentences never cut sharply
- the draft sounds too frictionless

Why it feels fake:

- human writing often contains useful rough edges

Fix:

- allow plain turns
- allow short sentences to stand alone

## 8. Helpful Assistant Cushioning

Symptom:

- "当然，这并不是说……"
- "值得注意的是……"
- "我们可以看到……"

Why it feels fake:

- the text sounds like it is managing the user's feelings instead of saying something

Fix:

- delete the buffer
- state the point directly

## 9. False Completeness

Symptom:

- the draft tries to mention every side, every caveat, every implication

Why it feels fake:

- most real writing is purpose-built, not omni-complete

Fix:

- keep only the caveats that matter for the actual decision or reading experience

## 10. Quote Card Syndrome

Symptom:

- every sentence tries to land as a polished maxim

Why it feels fake:

- humans do not naturally write in nonstop poster lines

Fix:

- make only the real high-pressure sentence carry the weight
- let the surrounding sentences stay plain

## 11. Forced Neutrality

Symptom:

- the draft hides behind equal-distance language even when the writer clearly has a view

Why it feels fake:

- real text often reveals angle and confidence level

Fix:

- choose a position
- or name the uncertainty directly

## 12. Flat Sentence Weight

Symptom:

- every sentence is equally long, equally serious, equally dense

Why it feels fake:

- humans write in bursts, not in perfect metronome rhythm

Fix:

- vary length
- vary emphasis
- let one sentence do less

## 13. Cosmetic Humanization

Symptom:

- adding "哈哈" or "我觉得" to otherwise robotic text

Why it feels fake:

- the structure is still machine-native

Fix:

- rewrite ordering, pacing, and density first
- add surface markers only if the scene supports them

## 14. Register Collapse

Symptom:

- the draft gets "more human" by becoming too slangy for the task

Why it feels fake:

- wrong scene match is still wrong, even if lively

Fix:

- keep the domain and audience intact
- adjust texture, not just loudness

## Fast Audit Prompts

When a draft feels off, ask:

1. Is it too complete for the job?
2. Did it hide the stance?
3. Did it explain what could stay assumed?
4. Did it sound more polished than a real person would bother to be?
5. Did it become "human" only at the word level, not the structure level?
